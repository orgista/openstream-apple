OpenStream third-party notices

Every component below reaches a shipped OpenStream binary and is listed, with
its full license text, on the in-app screen Settings > About > Third-Party
Notices (Resources/ThirdPartyNotices/<name>.txt). All LGPL components are
linked as dynamic frameworks, so they can be replaced by the user; the
corresponding source and build inputs are reachable through the pinned
package references in OpenStream's Package.resolved.
