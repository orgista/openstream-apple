# Changelog

Sideload builds for each release are attached to the matching GitHub release. iOS, tvOS and visionOS
downloads are unsigned IPAs (sign them yourself, e.g. with AltStore or Sideloadly); the Mac download is an
ad-hoc signed app (right-click › Open the first time).

## [1.0 (18)] - 2026-10-11

### iPhone and iPad
- New: Live redesign. Video sits edge to edge at the top, with an On Now list and a Watching pill under it,
  a fixed category row with Events, and one search button.
- New: Picture in Picture for Live.
- New: Live errors show one symbol, a short title naming the source, and one button.
- New: the iPhone hero uses textless key art; the title page has a 16:9 trailer header with a sound control.
- Fixed: iOS 18 navigation and VoiceOver, Settings wording, multiview keeps its channels.

### Apple TV
- Fixed: Top Shelf shows your titles instead of a blurry logo (open the app once, then check the shelf).
- Fixed: Set Up from iPhone imports on Apple TV.
- Fixed: Apple TV focus and the Guide sheet.
- New: Live errors show one symbol, a short title naming the source, and one button.

### Apple Vision Pro
- New: Live redesign with an On Now list and a category row with Events.
- New: Live errors show one symbol, a short title naming the source, and one button.

### Mac
- New: Live errors show one symbol, a short title naming the source, and one button.
- Fixed: iCloud sync cleanup and Settings wording.

### All platforms
- Playback engine updated.
