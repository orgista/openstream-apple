# Security policy

## Supported versions

Security fixes go into the latest release of OpenStream on the App Store and TestFlight, and into the `main` branch of
this repository. Older builds are not patched.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for security problems. Report them privately through GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/orgista/openstream-apple/security/advisories/new) for this
repository, or email support@orgista.com.

Include what you found, the steps to reproduce it, the affected platform and version, and the impact you expect. We
aim to acknowledge reports within 3 business days and to share a fix timeline within 10 business days. We will credit
you in the release notes unless you ask us not to.

## Scope

In scope: the OpenStream apps for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Mac and Apple Vision Pro, and the source in this repository.
Out of scope: third-party servers or services you connect to OpenStream (for example your media server or TV
provider), and issues that need a jailbroken or otherwise modified device.
